Privacy & Data Practices Privacy Policy For Alpha Intel's Cat · Effective date: October 25, 2026 This policy explains how Alpha Intel's Cat (“the Bot”) processes information when used in Discord servers. The Bot is designed for moderation, server administration, utility, and community management. 1. Information the Bot Processes Depending on the features used and the permissions granted by a server, the Bot may process information made available through Discord, such as Discord user, server, channel, and message IDs; message text needed to handle commands or moderation checks; usernames and member or role information needed for server-management functions; and information submitted through commands or interactions. The Bot should only access information needed for its available features and the permissions it has been granted. 2. Storage and Retention The Bot operator does not intentionally maintain a separate database or external files to store Discord user data. Information may be processed temporarily in memory while the Bot responds to commands or performs enabled functions. The operator does not intend to maintain a permanent archive of users’ messages. The Bot’s actual operating environment has not been specified in this policy. If a hosting provider or other infrastructure service is used, that provider may process technical data or retain operational logs under its own policies. The operator should review those practices and update this policy if they materially change. 3. How Information Is Used Information may be processed to respond to commands, perform moderation checks, help authorized moderators manage a server, manage roles or member-related events where supported, and maintain the Bot’s functionality and security. 4. Message Content and AI Training Message content may be processed when necessary for a command or a moderation feature that checks messages. The Bot operator does not use message content to train AI or machine-learning models. 5. Sharing The Bot operator does not intentionally sell personal information or maintain a separate user-data database for sharing. Information may be sent through Discord when necessary to perform an action, such as replying to a command or carrying out a moderation task. Discord and any infrastructure provider may process information independently under their own terms and privacy policies. 6. Third-Party Services The Bot operates through Discord. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord’s own terms and privacy policy: Discord Privacy Policy Discord Terms of Service 7. Security The operator should take reasonable steps to protect the Bot’s credentials, permissions, and operating environment. No online service or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials to the Bot. 8. User Choices and Privacy Requests Users can choose whether to interact with the Bot and may ask a server administrator to remove it from a server. The Bot may not provide a dedicated opt-out control for every kind of temporary processing. For privacy questions or requests, contact the operator using the email below. 9. Children’s Privacy The Bot is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users must meet Discord’s applicable age requirements and comply with Discord’s terms. 10. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated if the Bot’s features or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the same public URL. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made. 11.
