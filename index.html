# Privacy & Data Practices ## Privacy Policy **For cat · Effective date: October 27, 2026** This Privacy Policy explains how **cat** (“the Bot”), a Discord community management bot, processes information when used in Discord servers. The Bot is inspired by community management bots such as Dyno and is intended to support moderation, server administration, utility, and community management. ### 1. Information the Bot Processes Depending on the features used and the permissions granted by a server, the Bot may process information made available through Discord, including: - Discord user, server, channel, and message IDs. - Message text needed to process commands or perform enabled moderation checks. - Usernames, member information, and role information needed for server management. - Information submitted through commands or interactions. - Relevant Discord events needed to provide supported features. The Bot is intended to access only the information necessary for its available features and the permissions it has been granted. ### 2. Storage and Retention The Bot operator does not intentionally maintain a separate database or external files for storing Discord user data. Information may be processed temporarily in memory while the Bot responds to commands or performs enabled functions. The operator does not intend to maintain a permanent archive of users' messages. The Bot may run on infrastructure that processes technical information or operational logs. If a hosting provider or other infrastructure service is used, that provider may process or retain data under its own policies. This policy should be updated if the Bot's actual storage, hosting, or retention practices materially change. ### 3. How Information Is Used Information may be processed for the following purposes: - Responding to commands and interactions. - Performing enabled moderation checks. - Helping authorized moderators manage a Discord server. - Managing roles and member-related events where supported. - Supporting server administration and community management. - Maintaining the Bot's functionality, reliability, and security. ### 4. Message Content and AI Training Message content may be processed when necessary to handle supported text-based commands or provide moderation features that inspect messages. The Bot operator does not use message content to train AI or machine-learning models. ### 5. Sharing The Bot operator does not intentionally sell personal information or maintain a separate user-data database for sharing. Information may be transmitted through Discord when necessary to perform a requested or authorized action, such as replying to a command or carrying out a moderation task. Discord and any infrastructure provider may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies. ### 6. Third-Party Services The Bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policy: - [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) - [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) Any hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate the Bot may also have their own terms and privacy policies. ### 7. Security The operator should take reasonable steps to protect the Bot's credentials, permissions, source code, and operating environment. No online service or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should never send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials to the Bot. ### 8. User Choices and Privacy Requests Users can choose whether to interact with the Bot and may ask a server administrator to remove it from a server. The Bot may not provide a dedicated opt-out control for every type of temporary information processing. For privacy questions or requests concerning the Bot, users may contact the operator using the email address listed below. ### 9. Children's Privacy The Bot is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users must meet Discord's applicable age requirements and comply with Discord's Terms of Service. ### 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, operating environment, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same public URL whenever possible. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made. ### 11.
